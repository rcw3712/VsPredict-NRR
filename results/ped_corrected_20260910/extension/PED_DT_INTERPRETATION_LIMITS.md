# DT Distribution Shift — Interpretation Limits

The extreme DT mismatch (z=+7.85, KS=1.000) is **associated with** model failure but causation cannot be established from this dataset alone.

Possible sources of DT shift (cannot be confirmed without metadata):
- Different lithology or porosity between wells
- Different fluid type or saturation
- Different logging tool generation or acquisition program
- Environmental correction differences
- Different reservoir quality or diagenetic state

**Safe wording:** "associated with", "coincided with", "largest observed predictor mismatch"
**Avoid:** "DT shift caused failure", "geological heterogeneity caused the shift"
